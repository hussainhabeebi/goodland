Privacy Policy

How Goodland Blue Mist Resorts collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: 4 July 2026

1. Introduction

Goodland Blue Mist Resorts ("we", "us", "our"), located at Pottankadu, Bison Valley, Munnar, Idukki, Kerala – 685565, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website and how it is used when you enquire about or book a stay with us.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our booking form or contact us, you may voluntarily provide:

  • Full name and phone number
  • Preferred check-in and check-out dates
  • Number of guests and room preference
  • Selected add-on experiences (campfire, adventure park, jeep safari, food package)
  • Any additional message you choose to share

This website does not use a server-side database. The booking form on this site compiles the details you enter directly into a WhatsApp message, which is sent to our business number (+91 88483 58030) only when you choose to submit it. We do not automatically store, sell, or share this information with third parties.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to booking enquiries and confirm reservations
  • To communicate with you about your stay via WhatsApp, phone, or email
  • To improve our services and guest experience

4. WhatsApp Communication

Our booking and enquiry buttons open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message addressed to our business number. Messages sent via WhatsApp are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy and terms, which are governed by Meta Platforms, Inc. We recommend reviewing WhatsApp's privacy practices separately.

5. Cookies & Analytics

This website does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. Should this change in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any information shared with us directly (via phone, email, or WhatsApp). However, no method of transmission over the internet or mobile networks is completely secure.

7. Your Rights

You may request that we delete any personal information you've shared with us by contacting us directly at bluemistresorts@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and booking services are intended for use by adults arranging travel. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
