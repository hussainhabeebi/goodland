Our Story

A Misty Retreat in the Heart of Bison Valley

Pottankadu, Bison Valley, Munnar, Idukki — 685565, Kerala

Tucked between mist-laced tea hills and dense sholas, Goodland Blue Mist Resorts is a family-run getaway built for travellers who want the mountains without giving up comfort. Every morning here begins with fog rolling through the valley, and every evening ends around a fire.

Run personally by Shameer Muhammed and family, we take pride in warm hospitality, spotless rooms and experiences that actually get you outdoors — not just a view from the balcony.

  • Spacious rooms with modern interiors
  • Bonfire evenings under a starlit sky
  • On-site adventure park & jeep safaris
  • Home-style Kerala food packages
